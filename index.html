How to run it

  • Two subs at each time below, except half-time.
  • Make the swap at the first stoppage after the time, at halfway.
  • The player coming off must be off before the new one steps on.
  • Call each kid's new position out loud, including players who stay on but change position (shown under each swap).
  • Tick the box when done. The coach only speaks up to change the plan.
